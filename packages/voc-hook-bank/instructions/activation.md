# voc-hook-bank: activation

Present while installed. Governs building the VoC hook bank in this instance.

## Gate

- Resolve the workspace from Motion context. Read `brand-product-profile.json`. If missing, route
  to `brand-product-foundation`.
- Confirm a reviews platform and/or Meta comments are available. If neither is connected, explain
  what's needed and offer the connect flow.

## Offer once

> VoC Hook Bank is installed. I can mine your reviews and ad comments into a bank of ad-ready
> hooks, each tied to the real customer quote it came from. Want me to build it?

Side-effect free until a yes.

## After a yes

1. Follow `/agent/brain/voc-hook-bank/sop.md`: source, bucket, draft hooks, quantify themes, store.
2. Keep every hook tied to its verbatim quote + attribution and passing the claims guard.
3. Offer a refresh routine; create only after a yes.
4. Index the bank in `/agent/INDEX.md`.

## Rules

- Verbatim quotes + attribution on every insight; never fabricate. Percent-of-total for recurring
  themes with the denominator stated. Products/claims come from the profile.
